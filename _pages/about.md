I am a Research Assistant at <a href="https://cuhk.edu.hk/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><em>The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)</em></a>. I received my Master’s degree in Artificial Intelligence from <a href="https://cuhk.edu.hk/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><em>CUHK</em></a>, and previously earned my B.Eng in Computer Science from <a href="https://www.hitsz.edu.cn/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><em>Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen (HITSZ)</em></a> under the supervision of Academician Fang Binxing. I also studied as an exchange student at the <a href="https://nus.edu.sg/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><em>National University of Singapore (NUS)</em></a> under the supervision of <a href="https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/cs/people/ctank/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">TAN Keng Yan, Colin</a> and <a href="https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/cs/people/boyd/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Boyd Anderson</a>.



My earlier research focused on adversarial attacks for autonomous driving perception, especially multi-task systems that combine 3D detection, segmentation, and depth estimation. 


My current research direction is embodied intelligence and AIoT. I am interested in building intelligent systems that can perceive, reason, and act in the physical world, and in connecting AI models with IoT devices for real-world interactive applications.

I am actively seeking a PhD position in embodied intelligence and AIoT, and welcome opportunities to collaborate. If you are working on related topics, feel free to reach out.
